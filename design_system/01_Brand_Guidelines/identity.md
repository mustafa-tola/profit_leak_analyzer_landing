# Codvera Brand Identity — v1.0

## Positioning
**Name:** Codvera  
**Descriptor:** COD Reconciliation & Financial Intelligence  
**Tagline:** Every COD rupee, accounted for.  
**Voice:** Clear, credible, restrained, evidence-led, merchant-friendly.

## Visual principles
1. **Light first:** Cloud (#F5F8F7) canvas, white surfaces, subtle borders; never large dark navigation blocks as the default.
2. **Focused green:** Deep Forest (#103E36) for brand emphasis; Action Green (#087F62) for primary actions, selected navigation, verified status.
3. **Softer text:** Slate (#334B5B) for ordinary interface copy; darker ink (#192B32) when contrast requires it, particularly small type.
4. **Trustworthy data:** Verified expected/paid/outstanding are primary; provisional discrepancy estimates are secondary and labeled.
5. **Intentional density:** Tables are searchable and paginated; important details have expandable evidence.
6. **No misleading visualizations:** For a single run without history, show a compact honest empty state rather than simulated trends.

## Typography
- **Display / marketing:** Plus Jakarta Sans (fallback system sans).
- **Product UI / numbers:** Inter (fallback system sans), `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for money.
- Body text: 16px baseline, 1.5–1.65 line-height. Avoid shrinking supporting text to solve layout overflow.
- Financial amounts: emphasize hierarchy, allow responsive wrapping/truncation only with meaningful full-value access.

## Logo
An **original geometric interlocking-path mark**, evoking matching and verification, with a compact wordmark. Use `02_Logo_SVG/` for editable proposed vectors. Do not claim these are the same contours as the generated artwork.
- Primary horizontal brand on white.
- Reversed brand for occasional deep-forest banners.
- Single-color icon for small interfaces.
- Favicon should remain identifiable at 16/24/32px; inspect actual rendered sizes.
- Keep minimum clear space approximately equal to the icon stroke width around mark.

## Brand messaging
**Homepage:** Know where your COD money stands.  
**Description:** Reconcile courier settlements, identify payment discrepancies, and understand outstanding receivables in one financial workspace.  
**CTA:** Analyze COD payments.  
**Disclaimer:** Potential discrepancies are provisional findings, not confirmed financial loss.
